About this deck & reviews

Reviews use FSRS, the scheduler used by Anki, with a 90% target retention. Again means forgotten; Hard means recalled with effort; Good means recalled; Easy means effortless. Ratings set the next review date. “Already know it” sets a word aside for 60 days without inventing review history. You can find it in Explore the words or bring it back early; normal scheduling resumes after its next review. This app does not sync with Anki. About FSRS · Scheduler license

How progress works

Talk updates after a completed conversation, using evidence from your answers. Each skill can move forward one column per session; skills needing help move back one column, no lower than Practicing. Uncertain skills stay unchanged. Review the reasons and undo updates in Session history. Memorize uses FSRS reviews. Listen saves which words you understand while listening to songs.
